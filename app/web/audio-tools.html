LOCAL AUDIO

Test with your handheld

Speak on your handheld, release PTT, then listen to the replay. Your computer microphone is not used.

  1. Disconnect network links and stop any digital worker before testing.
  2. Use the same frequency and tones as the Pi. Confirm the antenna or load is attached.
  3. Arm the test, wait for Armed, then speak for 2–5 seconds and release PTT.

How the test works

D-STAR tests encode and decode your recording locally, then replay it over the analog radio. This checks codec sound, not digital RF interoperability. Comparisons use the same recording and gain, with a one-second gap.

Hardware recordings are limited to 5 seconds, other recordings to 10 seconds. The session expires after 40 seconds. Stop clears recordings and restores previous settings. No test starts automatically.
