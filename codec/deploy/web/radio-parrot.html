Handheld / SHARI radio test

Speak and listen on your handheld. This page only controls the Pi; no computer microphone or speaker is used.

D-Star mode records SHARI receive audio, encodes and decodes it locally, then plays it on your handheld. A/B plays the same original recording first, a one-second gap, then its D-Star version, with no automatic gain adjustment. This tests codec sound over your analog radio, not digital RF interoperability. Software/hardware A/B plays software first, a one-second gap, then the configured external vocoder, using the same recording and gain. Processing can take several seconds. The production D-Star worker must be stopped. Unavailable hardware cancels replay; there is no fallback.

  1. Disconnect all ALLMODE network/digital links.
  2. Keep the handheld's existing matching channel and tones. Confirm the RF channel and antenna/load.
  3. Select a test, check the box, and press Arm radio test. Wait for armed.
  4. Speak for 2–5 seconds and release PTT. Listen without keying again until the test finishes.

Loading status.

Maximum recording: 5 seconds for hardware tests; 10 seconds for software/analog tests. Hardware conversion may take several seconds before replay. Session limit: 40 seconds. Stop cancels recording/playback and clears the saved comparison. Closing this page expires the heartbeat. Links are inhibited during the test and previous settings restored afterward. Completed original/processed recordings remain locally until Stop/clear or the next codec test.
