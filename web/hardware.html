FIRST-RUN SETUP · SHARI HAT

Configure your SA818S radio

Save a draft, review it, then explicitly program the module.

1. Identify the hardware

Connect a suitable antenna or dummy load. Keep the node in maintenance mode with Asterisk and bridges stopped. Physically ensure PTT cannot be asserted by another controller. This page cannot measure or guarantee the PTT pin state.

Detection lists candidate ports without sending commands. Select the SHARI port; multiple ports require your choice. A handshake does not identify the module’s band.

Not checked

2. Choose settings

Kits4Hams baseline: squelch 6, volume 6, 91.5 Hz CTCSS, reverse burst closed, emphasis and both filters enabled. The sheet’s 446.1000 MHz is an example, not an automatic frequency choice.

Squelch 0 opens the receiver continuously. For this initial release, frequencies are restricted to 144–148 or 420–450 MHz in 2.5 kHz steps; local restrictions still apply. Reverse burst needs TX CTCSS.

3. Review & program

Validate settings to see the exact commands.

Programming is several separate writes. Failure can leave a partially changed radio; keep services stopped and reprogram the complete configuration. An acknowledgement is not an RF test.
